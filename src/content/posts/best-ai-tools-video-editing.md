"AI video tool" covers a much wider range of use cases than most lists admit. Avatar generation, short-clip creation, and traditional editing assistance are three different jobs, and the tools built for each barely overlap.

## For talking-head videos without filming: Synthesia or HeyGen

If you need a presenter-style video (training content, product explainers) without booking a camera or a voice actor, Synthesia and HeyGen both convert a script into an avatar-led video. Synthesia has the wider language and voice library; HeyGen tends to edge it on lip-sync realism and personal avatar cloning.

## For short AI-generated clips: Runway or Pika

For generating short video clips from text or an image, Runway offers more editing tools alongside generation (like green-screen removal), while Pika prioritizes speed and simplicity for quick social content. Neither is meant for long-form storytelling yet, clip lengths are still short.

## For editing existing footage faster: Descript

If you already have raw footage or a recorded call, Descript's transcript-based editing (cut the text, cut the clip) and automatic filler-word removal save far more time than avatar or generation tools, which don't touch existing recordings at all.

## The bottom line

Match the tool to the job: avatars for scripted presentations, generation tools for short novel clips, and Descript for editing what you already recorded.
