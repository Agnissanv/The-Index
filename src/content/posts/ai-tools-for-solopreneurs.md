Running a business alone means every hour spent on admin is an hour not spent on the work that actually makes money. The right AI tools don't replace that work, they clear out the parts around it.

## Notion AI, for keeping your own notes organized

If you already run your business out of Notion, its built-in AI Q&A can summarize meeting notes and answer questions using your own workspace, cutting down time spent digging through old notes.

## Otter.ai, for meetings you can't fully focus on

Solo founders take a lot of calls. Otter transcribes them live and generates a summary with action items afterward, so you're not trying to type notes and stay present in the conversation at the same time.

## Zapier AI, for the busywork between your tools

Most of a solopreneur's admin time is manually moving information between apps, a new form submission that should become a task, an invoice that should trigger a follow-up email. Zapier's AI can draft that automation from a plain description instead of building it step by step.

## Grammarly, for everything you send

Every email, proposal, and client message is representing your business alone. Grammarly's always-on checking catches tone and clarity issues before they go out, without needing a separate editing pass.

## Gamma, for the decks you don't have time to design

Client proposals and pitch decks eat hours you don't have. Gamma turns a rough outline into a designed deck in minutes, leaving you to edit rather than build from a blank page.

## The bottom line

None of these replace judgment, but together they remove hours of admin work per week, time better spent on clients or product.
