Most "free AI tools" lists mix in tools with a 7-day trial disguised as free. These don't, every tool here has a free tier you can use indefinitely, not just sample before paying.

## For everyday questions and writing: ChatGPT

ChatGPT's free tier covers most everyday writing, research, and brainstorming without hitting a wall constantly. It's the easiest starting point if you haven't tried an AI assistant yet.

## For research grounded in your own documents: Google NotebookLM

NotebookLM is completely free and answers questions strictly based on documents you upload yourself, useful for studying or research without worrying about made-up answers from the open web.

## For images with readable text: Ideogram

Most free image generators struggle badly with text inside images. Ideogram's free tier handles it well, useful for quick social graphics or posters without paying for a full design tool.

## For meeting notes: Otter.ai

Otter's free tier transcribes and summarizes a solid number of meeting minutes per month, enough for someone who takes a handful of calls a week without needing the paid plan.

## For voice generation: ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs' free tier gives you a real taste of its natural-sounding voice generation, enough characters to narrate a short video or test a project before deciding if you need more.

## The bottom line

Free tiers are genuinely usable for light, regular use, they're just not built for daily heavy use. Once a tool becomes part of your actual workflow, the paid tier is usually worth it.
